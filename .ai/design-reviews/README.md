# Design Reviews

Inputs and outcomes of design reviews — e.g., the decision briefs that resolved open questions and fed the [ADRs](../../docs/adr/README.md).

Archiving these here preserves the *reasoning trail* behind decisions without cluttering the authoritative ADRs. When a review produces a decision, the decision belongs in an ADR; the review material that led to it can be archived here and linked from the ADR if useful.

Suggested convention: `YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md`.
